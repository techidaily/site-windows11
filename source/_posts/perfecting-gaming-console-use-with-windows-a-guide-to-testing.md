## How to Test Your Gaming Controller on Windows

 Both Windows 10 and 11 include a tool that lets you test your gaming controller in a few easy steps. Here’s how you can use it to determine if your gaming controller needs calibrating.

1. Press **Win + S** to access the search menu.
2. Type **control panel** in the search box and press **Enter**.
3. Use the drop-down menu in the top right corner to change the view type to **Large icons**.
4. Select the **Devices and Printers** applet.
5. Right-click on your controller under **Devices** and select **Game controller settings** from the context menu.
6. Click the **Properties** button.
7. Switch to the **Test** tab.  
![Test Gaming Controller on Windows](https://static1.makeuseofimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/test-gaming-controller-on-windows.jpg)
8. Move the sticks in all directions and observe the movement on the screen. The indicator in the window should correspond to the direction you move the stick.

## How to Calibrate Your Gaming Controller Using the Control Panel

 By calibrating your gaming controller, you can fine-tune its sensitivity and responsiveness, resulting in better accuracy while gaming. You can calibrate your controller from the same menu where you initially tested it. Here are the steps you can follow.

1. [Open game controller settings](https://www.makeuseof.com/how-to-launch-game-controllers-settings-windows-11/) on Windows.
2. Select your controller from the list and click the **Properties** button.
3. Under the **Settings** tab, click the **Calibrate** button.
4. Click **Next** to initiate the calibration process.  
![Calibrate Gaming Controller on Windows](https://static1.makeuseofimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/calibrate-gaming-controller-on-windows.jpg)
5. Perform specific actions provided by the calibration wizard, such as moving the analog sticks or pressing buttons, to complete the process.

## How to Calibrate Your Gaming Controller via Steam

 Using the Control Panel isn’t the only way to calibrate your gaming controller on Windows. If you have installed Steam on your PC, you can also [use Steam’s Controller Configurator](https://www.makeuseof.com/steam-controller-configurator-explained/) to calibrate your controller. It supports a wide range of controllers, including both popular gaming controllers and generic input devices.

 To calibrate your gaming controller via Steam's Controller Configurator, use these steps:

1. Open the Steam client on your PC.
2. Click the **Steam** menu in the top left corner and select **Settings**.
3. Use the left pane to switch to the **Controller** tab.
4. Click the **General Controller Settings** button.
5. Select your controller under **Detected Controllers**.
6. Click the **Calibrate** button.
7. Click the **Start Full Autocalibration** button.
8. Follow the on-screen instructions to move the analog sticks in different directions and complete the calibration process.  
![Calibrate Gaming Controller Using Steam](https://static1.makeuseofimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/calibrate-gaming-controller-using-steam.jpg)

## How to Reset Controller Calibration to Default Settings on Windows

 If the sensitivity of your gaming controller feels off, it's possible that the calibration settings are to blame. In that case, you can consider resetting the controller calibration and start over. This should help resolve any minor sensitivity and calibration problems that might be responsible for the issue.

 To reset controller calibration to default settings, use these steps:

1. Press **Win + R** to [open the Run dialog box](https://www.makeuseof.com/windows-open-run-command-dialog-box/).
2. Type **joy.cpl** in the box and press **Enter** to open the game controller settings.
3. Select your controller and click the **Properties** button.
4. Under the **Settings** tab, click the **Reset to Default** button.
5. Click **OK**.  
![Reset Gaming Controller Calibration on Windows](https://static1.makeuseofimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/reset-gaming-controller-calibration-on-windows.jpg)
